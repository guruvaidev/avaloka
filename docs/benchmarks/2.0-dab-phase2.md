* **Rate:** Groq's own model page (console.groq.com/docs/model/openai/gpt-oss-120b, checked 2026-10-31): 0.15 in / 0.075 cached / 0.60 out per 1M tokens. `run_cell.sh` checked it against the new Groq table (`benchmarks/catalog/provider_rates.json`) and printed OK.

## What each fix did in this run

* **(a) Numeric check on label questions.** The numeric finding fired once, on yelp:1, a question that really asks for a number ("What is the average rating …"). In rc7 it fired on the label question crmarenapro:3; here it did not. On gpt-oss rc7 the finding cost no pass outright. The cell where (a) matters is Haiku (crmarenapro:3 and :9 were discarded as review_failed in both earlier runs), so its effect is still unmeasured.
* **(b) Answers sent to a tool that doesn't exist.** 23 of 54 trials returned their answer through a phantom `commentary`/`json` call that Groq refused and the mission accepted. 9 of those passed. Without (b), every one of them would have been re-prompted and, after two rejections, ended as provider_error. Three wins are clean (b) recoveries of rc7 provider errors (crmarenapro:4, googlelocal:4, music_brainz_20k:2). Two losses (crmarenapro:2, :3) are wrong answers delivered through the same path.
* **(c) Tool-output trim.** No `context_length_exceeded` (rc7: 2). History was compacted in 3 trials. The two former overflow queries now fail differently: DEPS_DEV_V1:2 at max iterations, PATENTS:3 at the tool-error limit. stockindex:3 passed with one compaction.
* **(d) Strategy switch after three repeats.** Fired in 3 trials (PANCANCER_ATLAS:1, yelp:2, yelp:7). None recovered a pass: yelp:2 and :7 repeated again and ended as before, and PANCANCER_ATLAS:1 ran out of iterations. Three-strikes endings fell from 8 to 2, but most of that drop is answers arriving earlier through (b).
